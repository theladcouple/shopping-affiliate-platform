Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 21, 2026

1. Introduction

The Lad Couple respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit our website and how that information is used.

2. Information We Collect

Our website does not currently require visitors to create accounts or submit personal details such as their name, phone number, email address, residential address or payment information through registration or checkout forms.

However, we use website analytics tracking that may collect the following technical and usage information:

  • A randomly generated visitor identifier stored in browser local storage.
  • A randomly generated session identifier stored in session storage.
  • Pages visited on our website.
  • Products viewed on our website.
  • Clicks on shopping or affiliate buttons.
  • The shopping platform associated with an outbound click.
  • Traffic source, such as Instagram, Facebook, YouTube or direct visits.
  • Campaign information when included in a website link.
  • The date and time an analytics event is recorded.

3. How We Use This Information

We use analytics information to:

  • Understand website traffic and visitor activity.
  • Identify popular products and pages.
  • Understand which social platforms bring visitors to our website.
  • Measure interactions with shopping links.
  • Improve our website and its user experience.

4. Browser Storage and Analytics

Our analytics system uses browser local storage to retain a randomly generated visitor identifier and session storage to retain a session identifier.

Analytics events are sent to our website backend and stored in a Cloudflare D1 database. The information stored includes event type, identifiers, traffic source, page path, product information, affiliate platform and event timestamp.

The analytics tracking code currently used on our website does not specify an automatic deletion period for these database records. Records may be retained until they are deleted or are no longer required.

5. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, Myntra and other third-party shopping websites.

When you click an external link, you leave our website. Those platforms have their own privacy policies, cookies, data collection practices and terms. We do not control how they collect or use your information.

Please review the privacy policy of the relevant platform before sharing personal or payment information with it.

6. Hosting and Service Providers

Our website uses Cloudflare services for website hosting and database functionality. Cloudflare may process technical information as necessary to provide, maintain and secure these services, subject to its applicable policies.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information handled by our website. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Choices

You can manage or clear browser local storage and session storage through your browser settings. Clearing these identifiers may affect analytics tracking on future visits.

You may contact us to ask questions or request information about data associated with your visit. We will consider requests in accordance with applicable laws and our ability to identify the relevant records.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. Please contact us if you believe a child has submitted personal information to us.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, analytics practices or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

11. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions, contact: theladcouple@gmail.com
